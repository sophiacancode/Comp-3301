#Proposed Change 

Add a Google Map showing the location of my hometown. Users will be able to view the location, zoom in and out, and explore the surrounding area.

## Reasons for the Change

The stakeholder wants to make the project more informative, interactive, and appealing. The map will help users understand where my hometown is located without having to search for it separately.

## How This Change Will Add Value

The map will improve the user experience by providing a visual and interactive map of my hometown. It will make the project more engaging while providing useful information.

## How This Change Will Affect the Constraints

Time: Low impact - A few hours needed to add, configure, and test the map.
Quality: Positive impact - The map adds useful information and improves the project’s usability.
Budget: Low impact - A standard Google Map should require little to no additional development cost.

Overall Impact: This is a low impact change that requires minimal additional time and budget while improving the project’s functionality.
